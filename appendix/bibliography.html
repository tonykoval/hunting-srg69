Appendix C Bibliography

  1. A. E. Brouwer, Parameters of Strongly Regular Graphs (online table), www.win.tue.nl/~aeb/graphs/srg/. The "$?$" entry for $(69,20,7,5)$ is the subject of this book.
  2. A. E. Brouwer, A. M. Cohen, A. Neumaier, Distance-Regular Graphs, Springer, 1989.
  3. A. E. Brouwer, W. H. Haemers, Spectra of Graphs, Springer, 2012.
  4. M. Behbahani, C. Lam, Strongly regular graphs with non-trivial automorphisms, Discrete Math. 311 (2011) 132–144. (The orbit-matrix / prototype method of Chapter 2.)
  5. M. Behbahani, On Strongly Regular Graphs, PhD thesis, Concordia University, 2009.
  6. D. Crnković, S. Rukavina, Construction of block designs admitting an abelian automorphism group, Metrika 62 (2005) 175–183. (Column orbit matrices.)
  7. K. Gritsenko, On strongly regular graph with parameters $(65,32,15,16)$, 2021 (arXiv:2102.05432). A nearby open case resolved by construction.
  8. S. Shpectorov, X. Zhao, Strongly regular graphs with parameters $(85,14,3,2)$ do not exist, 2025 (arXiv:2504.02449). A nearby open case resolved by non-existence.
  9. P. J. Cameron, Strongly regular graphs, in Topics in Algebraic Graph Theory, CUP, 2004.
  10. T. Koval, There is no strongly regular graph with parameters $(69,20,7,5)$, write-up, September 2026: docs/paper/srg69.tex in github.com/tonykoval/orbit-gen. In preparation for review; not yet published or peer-reviewed. The full proof outlined in Chapter 7.
  11. R. E. Borcherds, The Leech lattice and other lattices, PhD thesis, University of Cambridge, 1985 (arXiv:math/9911195); and Tables of lattices, math.berkeley.edu/~reb/lattices/. The $665$ odd unimodular lattices of dimension $25$ behind the determinant-$8$ catalogue of Chapter 7.
  12. A. E. Brouwer, A. R. Calderbank, An Erdős–Ko–Rado theorem for regular intersecting families of octads, Graphs Combin. 2 (1986) 309–316. Used for the octad case of the Leech lattice (§7.3).
  13. J. H. Conway, N. J. A. Sloane, Sphere Packings, Lattices and Groups, 3rd ed., Springer, 1999. The Leech lattice, $\mathrm{Co}_0$, the Niemeier lattices and their glue codes.
  14. V. V. Nikulin, Integral symmetric bilinear forms and some of their applications, Math. USSR Izv. 14 (1980) 103–167. Discriminant forms and genera (§7.2).
  15. N. Wetzler, M. J. H. Heule, W. A. Hunt, DRAT-trim: efficient checking and trimming using expressive clausal proofs, in SAT 2014, LNCS 8561, 422–429. The checker for every UNSAT proof of engine 2 (§7.6, §7.8).
  16. A. Biere, K. Fazekas, M. Fleury, M. Heisinger, CaDiCaL, Kissat, Paracooba, Plingeling and Treengeling entering the SAT Competition 2020, Proc. SAT Competition 2020. The kissat solver (version 4.0.4) of engine 2.
  17. B. D. McKay, A. Piperno, Practical graph isomorphism, II, J. Symbolic Comput. 60 (2014) 94–112. nauty, used for pool symmetry groups and the Leech octad group order.
  18. Software of Chapter 7: The PARI Group, PARI/GP 2.15.4; C. Fieker, W. Hart, T. Hofmann, F. Johansson, Nemo/Hecke: computer algebra and number theory packages for Julia, ISSAC 2017, 157–164; L. Perron, F. Didier, CP-SAT, Google OR-Tools (engine 1).
  19. The companion volume, Hunting SRG(37) (github.com/tonykoval/hunting-srg37), on the related catalog-completeness question for the conference parameters $(37,18,8,9)$.
  20. Companion search engine: github.com/tonykoval/orbit-gen — the orbit-matrix enumerator, lifter, PDS search and Gram-PSD filter used throughout.
